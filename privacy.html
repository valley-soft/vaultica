Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026 · Vaultica 1.0.0

Download PDF ↓

1. Scope

This policy explains how Vaultica, a Windows application published by ValleySoft, handles information in the current release. Vaultica is designed as a local-first application. It does not require a Vaultica account.

2. Information Vaultica stores locally

Your notes, password-vault entries, tags, dates, layouts, settings, managed attachments, note history, and recovery snapshots are stored on your Windows device. Backups are written to locations you choose. Some metadata—including tags, dates, layout information, and attachments—may remain unencrypted in the live local workspace even when a note is protected.

3. Protected information

PIN-protected note text and password entries are encrypted locally before persistence. ValleySoft does not receive or retain your PIN, vault secrets, or backup passphrase. Global PIN recovery requires a recovery kit you created in advance; per-note PINs cannot be recovered by ValleySoft.

4. Collection and transmission

Vaultica 1.0.0 contains no ValleySoft analytics, advertising, telemetry, user-account, or cloud-synchronization service. ValleySoft does not sell application data. Local screenshot capture, image recognition, and Windows system OCR are processed on the device.

5. User-initiated external actions

When you choose to open a detected website, map address, email address, phone number, or local file, Windows may send that information to the external application or service you selected. Microsoft Store acquisition, licensing, updates, and crash information may be handled by Microsoft under Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices.

6. Exports and backups

Readable exports such as Markdown or PDF are not encrypted unless you separately protect them. Encrypted full-vault backups use the passphrase you provide. You control their destination, retention, and deletion.

7. Retention and deletion

Information remains on your device until you delete it through Vaultica, remove associated backup files, or choose to remove local application data during uninstall. Operating-system backups, filesystem recovery tools, or copies you created may retain data independently.

8. Security limitations

No local security measure can guarantee protection against every device compromise, malware infection, weak PIN, or loss of physical access. Keep Windows updated, use a strong device sign-in method and PIN, and maintain verified backups.

9. Children's privacy

Vaultica is a general-purpose productivity application and is not directed to children. Because the current release has no ValleySoft account or telemetry service, ValleySoft does not knowingly collect children's personal information through Vaultica.

10. Changes to this policy

ValleySoft may update this policy when Vaultica's information practices or legal requirements change. Material revisions will be identified by a new effective date and made available on the official Vaultica website.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to valleysoftdev29@gmail.com or through the Vaultica support page.
